# Pre-trade gate

Adapted from the pre-trade discipline idea in tradermonty/claude-trading-skills (MIT). A decision without a plan is refused by the reviewer.

## The gate (all must be true)
1. **A reason in one sentence** that is about the business or the price, with a dated source or a calculation id behind every number.
2. **Evidence against, looked for and written down.** A decision with no evidence against it was not looked at.
3. **The alternatives named:** waiting, holding, the index, a smaller amount.
4. **What would prove it wrong:** a price, a date, a filing. A HOLD or WAIT names what reopens it.
5. **Inside the rails and the breaker:** 10% a company, 25% a sector, no new buying while the circuit breaker is on.
6. **After charges and tax:** the move is large enough to matter.
7. **Not chasing:** the price has not just risen on news the market already knows.
8. **Fresh:** the facts are from today's record, not from an earlier investigation that has since changed (memory says what changed).

## For the reviewer
Approve only what passes all eight. Ask for a revision naming the item that fails. A decision that does nothing (HOLD, WAIT) is acceptable with weaker evidence than a trade, but still needs 3 and 4. Say plainly when you are objecting to the analysis and not the decision.
