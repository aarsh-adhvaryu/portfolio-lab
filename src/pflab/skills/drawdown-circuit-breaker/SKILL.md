# Drawdown circuit breaker

Adapted from the drawdown halt idea in tradermonty/claude-trading-skills (MIT). The halt itself is code: while the account is 12% or more below its own high (outside money taken out), BUY and ADD are refused. Selling, holding and waiting stay open.

## What you do while it is on
1. **Find out why before doing anything:** which holdings and decisions caused the fall; was it the market, a sector, one name, or a decision this system made? Use the postmortem lessons.
2. **Do not try to win it back.** No larger positions, no new ideas to make up the loss.
3. **Check the rails.** A fall that left one name above its weight limit is a reason to trim, not to hold.
4. **Decide what the account is for again:** long term, 3+ years; after tax and charges; a smaller worst fall than the Nifty 500. A fall deeper than the index's means the account is losing at its own job.
5. **State the condition for resuming** (the account back above the line, or a named change) in the HOLD or WAIT you record.

## Near the line (8% to 12% below)
Treat every new idea as needing a smaller size and a clearer wrong-price. Say that the breaker is near.
