# Weekly performance digest

Adapted from the weekly performance digest idea in tradermonty/claude-trading-skills (MIT). The figures come from the paper book and its record (`paper_book`); you write the words. Plain language: the reader is not a trader.

## Say, in this order
1. **Where it stands:** value, the week's change, the change since the start (outside money taken out), against the Nifty 500 over the same days. If it is behind, say by how much.
2. **What it did:** each decision of the week, one line, with its claim and whether it is due for scoring yet.
3. **What matured:** claims scored this week, how many held, partly held, or did not hold.
4. **What it learned:** the lessons that changed, and one thing it will do differently.
5. **What it is waiting for:** each HOLD and WAIT with the condition that reopens it.
6. **The gate:** how many sessions of the 40, and whether it is ahead of the Nifty 500 with a smaller worst fall.

## Never
Round a loss into a gain; hide a refused decision; give a number that is not in the record.
