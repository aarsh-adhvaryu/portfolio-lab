# Exposure posture

Adapted from the exposure-coach idea in tradermonty/claude-trading-skills (MIT), for NSE. Posture is a range, not a forecast.

## How to reason
1. **Measure first (code):** weights by company, sector and kind (shares, ETFs, gold); cash; the Nifty 500's distance below its one-year high; the account's own drawdown.
2. **Market state, from facts you can cite:** near its high (under 5% below), a pullback (5 to 15%), deep (over 15%). The scorecard's lessons say how decisions in each state have held.
3. **Name a range for shares** (for example 70 to 85% of the account) and say what moves it up or down. Gold and cash are the other two parts; gold is a diversifier, not a bet.
4. **Use cash by a rule stated in advance:** in tranches, on named conditions (a fall to a level, a result), never all at once and never because it is idle. Doing nothing is a decision if you say why.
5. **Concentration:** list holdings over 8%; for each, say why it is allowed to stay.

## Never
A posture from one indicator; a target that ignores the account's own drawdown; a claim about the market that has no dated source.
