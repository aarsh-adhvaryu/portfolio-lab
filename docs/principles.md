# Research principles

1. **The charter is law.** A strategy's rules, sweep and success criteria
   are written before its first run. After lock, changes go through the
   change log and invalidate prior results.
2. **Periods are sealed by the loader, not by discipline.** Data after a
   period's end never reaches the engine. Sealed periods require a written
   reason and are journaled. There is one reveal.
3. **Sweeps are stability checks, not menus.** Every row runs together.
   No row is promoted to "the strategy" after seeing results.
4. **Verdicts are computed.** `classify` applies the charter's arithmetic.
   INCONCLUSIVE is a real outcome and gets a written note, not a retry.
5. **Point-in-time or it does not exist.** Every feature is computed from
   rows dated on or before the decision date. Fundamentals and
   announcements are keyed by publication timestamp.
6. **Survivorship is a data error.** The universe includes every name that
   ever traded, including the ones that went to zero.
7. **Taxes and costs are in the objective**, not a footnote. Primary
   metrics are after-tax, after-cost, liquidation-adjusted.
8. **Benchmarks run through the same engine**, same SIP, same taxes.
9. **Artifacts are immutable and content-addressed.** A run's identity is
   the hash of its config, data and code. Re-running verifies; it does not
   overwrite.
10. **LLMs never decide.** They extract, classify text, and explain.
    Anything an LLM produces that feeds a decision is a point-in-time
    feature with a timestamp, and the LLM's training cutoff is recorded.
11. **A single backtest is not evidence.** Neither is one live year.
    The live year tests the machine; the sealed history tests the rules.
