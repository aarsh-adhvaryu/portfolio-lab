## Missions, workers and the assistant (S68)
One request becomes a **mission** (`hq/missions.py`, tables `hq_missions`, `hq_artifacts`, and the new `hq_tasks` columns case_id, role, depends_on, input_version, checkpoint_json, artifacts_json, lease_owner, lease_until, wait_kind, resume_condition; migration 52): a case (the parent investigation id) plus board tasks for a coordinator, research and quant workers and an independent reviewer (`hq/workers.py`, the governed tool loop `converse`). `POST /api/investigations` (and the assistant's `start_investigation`) creates one; `drive_missions` (HQ round) moves it on from the store: planning, working, reviewing (at most two revision rounds), synthesising, done, or blocked with the reason. A task is **done when the artifacts it promised exist** (`hq_artifacts`: evidence, calc, scenario, experiment, review, plan, answer), not when a model stops; an unmet promise is told to the worker twice, then the task fails with the reason. A worker may call only its role's tools (`Ctx.allowed`); `request_artifact` parks it until another worker's task is done. Waits are named and cost no attempt: the budget (`wait_kind` budget, resume time), the provider (back-off, bounded), the sandbox (a missing Docker is a wait, never a host run), another task. `GET /api/investigations` and `GET /api/cases/{id}` carry the card: goal, owner, current action, outputs, blocker, last useful progress, next trigger, snapshot id.
**Spend policy** (`hq/governor.py`, `GET /api/policy`): day limits, passes a day, layer shares and tool caps are configuration (`api_budget.toml [policy]`, read on every call); `[acceptance]` is a bounded window with its own cap, ending by itself; every call is tagged `api_calls.policy` and `his_order` (migration 51). A pass that stops part-way is `stopped`; its cases wait (`waiting_budget`, `waiting_provider`) with a resume time.
**Evaluation contracts** (`learner/contracts.py`, migration 53): `index_timing` and `deployment` for sandbox strategy experiments; a hypothesis no contract fits is `unsupported` and never run. Trusted, reproducible calculations (`quant/trusted.py`): `quant/policies.py` (deployment policies across fall, decline, recovery and no-crash paths: a hypothetical scenario with visible assumptions) and `quant/ipo_policy.py` (IPO sell or hold on listing history, development 2016-2019, validation 2020-2021, baseline sell at the close).
**Kite estimate** (`tax/kite_estimate.py`, shown by the `tax_plan` tool and the IPO study): shares Kite shows beyond the ledger (a new allotment before its tradebook and depository statement) get a provisional tax figure labelled "estimate, from Kite": Kite's quantity and average price, the short-term rate of `configs/tax_policy_in.toml` assumed, a loss giving no tax; never a lot, never written to the ledger, replaced by the engine's figure when the statements arrive.
**Files**: a dropped file's state is received, working, ready, in the ledger/graph/store, kept, failed or **blocked** (a locked file is kept under `pm/inbox/locked`, asked about once, and opens itself when its password is saved); only read files count as evidence for a request, whenever they arrived. **Secrets** (`scrub.py`): token-shaped strings are masked in job results, stored rows and logs.
**Autonomy** (`missions.scan`, in the HQ round when paid work is allowed): a signal the stores show (a material document about a held or watched name, an IPO allotment, a hypothesis to decide) is adopted as a mission with the reason it was chosen and what would justify revisiting (`why_chosen` on the card; the case's `chosen` log line); an unchanged signal is not adopted twice, a day's starts are bounded (3, twice that inside the acceptance window) and Pause stops it; a finished mission whose review day has come is reopened (`missions.reopen`, the same investigation, `cases.due` leaves missions to it); a blocked mission can be retried (`POST /api/investigations/{id}/retry`).
**The dashboard** (S68 redesign, `static/index.html`, `app.js`, `ui.js`, `world.js`): a left rail (Assistant, World, Portfolio, Research, Inbox and files, System; Settings below) with sub-views (Portfolio: overview, holdings and lots, watch list; Research: investigations, plans and ideas, simulations, knowledge graph; System: now, limits and spend, schedule). The Assistant is home: the one persistent thread, with the live work (each investigation's owner, current action, outputs, blocker, next trigger), what needs him and today's figures beside it; on every other screen it is one click away with the screen as context. The Command centre, the AI centre's chat modes and the Schedule tab as a screen are gone. A holding with no purchase record shows its cost as unknown and is left out of the gain total (`views.holdings`); a graph label is never "nan". **The World draws roles, not runners** (`roster.ROLES`, `roster.roles()`, `world.agents_view`): Brain, Assistant, Coordinator, Research, Quant, Reviewer, Readers, Learner, Memory and graph, Portfolio, Messenger, Security; each node adds up its members' tasks and says what it is made of; an order typed on a role goes to its `order_to` runner (Coordinator starts an investigation; Reviewer takes work only from investigations). A calculation that failed before any generated code ran is no longer reported as "uncontained" (`workshop.Run.contained` is None, not False).
**The assistant** (`app/assistant.py`, `GET/POST /api/assistant`): one persistent thread that discusses, runs calculations and scenarios, starts missions and receives their progress (`notify`, once per state); `tools/assistant_eval.py` is its synthetic calibration set; `tools/rehearse.py` runs a mission on an isolated synthetic profile.

## The workflow made explicit (S69, migration 54)
**One approval, then execution.** A request he makes becomes a mission whose first plan waits for his yes (`hq/plans.py`; `api_budget.toml [policy] plan_approval = "investor" | "none"`): the tasks exist with owners and dependencies as `needs_approval` and run only after `POST /api/investigations/{id}/approve` (button, or "yes" to the assistant, whose `decide_plan` checks his own words). Work the system chooses itself (autonomy, review days) is inside his standing objectives and asks nothing. **Numbered instructions** (`hq_plan_versions`: drafting, proposed, approved/auto, superseded, rejected): a chat amendment (`amend_investigation`, `POST /api/investigations/{id}/amend`) is the next version and persists with no release; unstarted tasks of the older version are replaced (said in the log), running ones finish and are re-checked, the coordinator replans only what changed; an amendment that changes the question or touches an amount or rule of his money waits for his yes again, a refinement does not. Every task carries the version it ran under (`hq_tasks.instruction_version`); a review older than the version in force is ignored and asked again, and the reviewer is told which tasks ran under which version. **Strategy versions** (`hq_policy`): a proposed change sits beside the active one and becomes active only by his press.
**Parallel work.** `board.claim` takes the oldest due task atomically (process lock plus `UPDATE ... RETURNING`); `hq/pool.py` runs 3 worker threads that take worker tasks while the HQ round, the chat and the other agents go on; `board.run_parallel` does the same inline where there is no pool (tests, the verifier's worker). A task waiting on another is parked, never queued. `store.next_id` does not hand one id to two threads; `llm.call` checks the limits and reserves the call's estimate under one lock, and each paid call carries its `task_id` (`api_calls.task_id`), so a mission's spend is the sum of its tasks' calls, not a time window. `Desk.cursor()` is a new cursor per call.
**Connections repaired.** The reviewer's verdict is one word everywhere (`missions.verdict_of`: the store kept `approved`, the state machine compared `approve`, so an approval was published as a rejection); the answer carries the review's real verdict. A registered experiment is `registered`, not a result: the mission waits (`missions._experiments_pending`), `missions.drive_experiments` runs it (the pool's first thread; local model writes the candidate, Docker runs it) instead of waiting for the nightly cycle, and the result enters the case as an `experiment_result` artifact and evidence, with a short quant task to say what it means, before any review. A spent mission budget PARKS the unfinished tasks (`waiting`, `mission_budget`, with how to resume: raise `[policy] mission_rupees` or `POST /api/investigations/{id}/allow`) and no answer is made from half the work. The same finding twice is one piece of evidence (`cases.same_evidence`; the merge is logged and is not a fault), the same source with the opposite stance is kept as a contradiction. A monitoring wish is executable only as a trigger (`hq/triggers.py`: price_below, price_above, move_pct, new_document, review_on; `submit_answer(triggers_json)`; unsupported kinds are refused with the reason and the answer says it has no executable monitor); the HQ round checks them, each fires once, a finished investigation is reopened.
**New files wake the right investigation** (`hq/arrivals.py`, HQ round, where paid work is allowed): a read file is first merged by code if it is already evidence; else the LOCAL model is asked one bounded question (which investigation, and new_information / contradicts / duplicate / unrelated), its JSON is validated in code, invalid output is asked again once with the error named, then a word-overlap rule decides if clear, else the leading investigation's coordinator (the cloud) does; every decision is in `hq_routing` (`GET /api/routing`). New information or a contradiction is added as evidence and the investigation is reopened (finished), given a research task (running), and its open proposals are marked for review; at most 4 reopenings a day by arrivals.
**Recommendations** (`hq/proposals.py`, `hq_proposals`, `hq_proposal_log`; `GET /api/proposals`, screen Research > Recommendations): BUY, ADD, HOLD, REDUCE, SELL, DIVERSIFY, WAIT. The model gives direction and reasons; code computes price and its date, cash, size, charges (`pm/costs.py`), weights before and after, proceeds and the tax effect from the lots (`tax/plan.Engine` + `tax/proposed.proposed_sale`); an acquisition cost not on record makes the tax UNKNOWN (never zero); an incomplete ledger (open known gaps or unexplained broker differences) gives direction and evidence but no size and no tax; HOLD/WAIT/DIVERSIFY refuse to be stored without a concrete condition for looking again; every proposal carries its missing inputs, assumptions, record snapshot, strategy version, plan version and review status (the independent reviewer's verdict reaches the case's proposals). Discuss, amend (a new version), accept, reject, withdraw are logged; accepting records that HE will act (no order, no GTT). `reconcile` (HQ round) matches an accepted proposal to the trades the ledger then holds, by a stable key (account, day, company, side, quantity, price, document reference; ledger event numbers are not kept across rebuilds), once, partial fills stay partial; the investigation is told and reopened to look at the new position, other open proposals on the company are marked for review.
**The screens**: the investigation card and its dialog show the plan versions and their status, each task with owner, state in words (a waiting task says what resumes it), dependencies, instruction version, times and outputs, the executable watches, experiments not yet finished (REGISTERED, not a result), the budget and "allow more", an amend box, and the recommendations; Research > Recommendations lists proposals and the local model's routing decisions.

## Facts the code relies on
- **Adjustment.** NSE's bhavcopy `PREVCLOSE` is NOT adjusted on ex-dates (450 of 451 large down-gaps 2012-2020), so split and bonus factors come from
  the corporate-actions feed and are verified on known events (`tests/test_adjustment_known_events.py`); `adj_close` in the panel uses them.
- **Index membership is point-in-time or not used.** NSE's circular series covers Nifty 200 and 500 only in 2006-2008; membership tables are built
  from archived factsheet/change PDFs (`build_index_membership.py`); names it cannot resolve are written to `PFLAB_DATA_DIR\index_membership\unresolved.md`
  for him to check. Never use today's list over a past period (survivorship).
- **BSE results** come from the integrated XBRL filings (standalone and consolidated) dated by filing time; shareholding by filing date, never the quarter end.
- **Tax rules** are dated, sourced rows (`configs/tax/*.toml`); coverage of capital gains starts at transfer date 2024-07-23 and a lookup with no row raises
  (`RuleGap`), never a default. Foreign-tax rows are all `secondary` (official wording seen in search results) until a primary check (due 2027-01-01 or the
  first remittance). The new-regime slab table for the year ending 2026-03-31 is not read: regime tax raises for that year; the Quicko pack does not need it.
- **Schedule 112A layout** is the department's CSV instruction (`configs/forms/itr_112a_layout.toml`, read 2026-10-28); the template's own header row and a
  possible column 1b are checked at filing time. Quicko files his return; the system prepares and reconciles, it files nothing.
- **Small-numbers rule:** results under 20 observations carry their count and no conclusion; probabilities are shown with their error.
