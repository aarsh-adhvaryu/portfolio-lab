# E1 — Momentum SIP with band rebalancing and trend filter (NSE equities)

Status: **LOCKED** 2026-09-17 (see §15). Changes go only through the change
log (§15), and any change invalidates every result produced before it.

## 1. Purpose

A personal, tax-aware, monthly-SIP equity portfolio over liquid NSE stocks.
E1 is the baseline that every later experiment (E2 events, E3 ML/graph)
must beat under the identical harness. It is deliberately simple. Its job
is not to be clever; its job is to be the thing cleverness must beat.

Private-use decision rule: if E1 does not beat the Nifty 200 Momentum 30
index fund after tax by the margin in §13, the right action is to buy that
fund and stop. Write that down now, before any run.

## 2. Universe (recomputed at every month-end M, from data dated <= M)

- Source: NSE cash-market daily bhavcopy, series `EQ` only.
- Security key: **ISIN**. Symbol changes never create a new security.
- Eligible on M if all hold:
  - instrument type is STK (ETFs, InvITs and REITs are excluded; they
    trade under series EQ but are not stocks)
  - at least 250 trading sessions of history (about 12 months listed)
  - ranked in the top 500 by mean daily traded value over the trailing
    126 sessions
  - close on M > 10.00
  - not flagged by the plausibility checks in §9
- Ranking by traded value (not market cap) avoids needing point-in-time
  shares outstanding.

## 3. Signal

- `MOM(M) = close(M − 21 sessions) / close(M − 252 sessions) − 1`
  (12-1 momentum: twelve months lookback, most recent month skipped).
- Adjusted closes (splits/bonuses). Ranked descending within the eligible
  universe. Rank 1 = strongest.

## 4. Holdings rules

- Target names `N = 25`.
- Entry candidates on M: non-held eligible names with rank <= 25.
- Exit: a held name is sold in full at the next session's open if its rank
  on M is > 50 (the band), or it is no longer eligible, or it is delisted
  or suspended (§9).
- The band (25 in, 50 out) is the turnover control. Turnover is STCG.

## 5. Cash flows and rebalancing

- Initial capital: config `initial_cash` (default 0).
- SIP 50,000 credited on the first session of each calendar month before
  the buy list executes. Config `sip_amount`.
- Buys: on the first session of the month, at the open, for at most 6
  names with the largest shortfall versus equal-weight target. Target
  value per name = (equity value + investable cash) / 25. Integer shares:
  `qty = floor(min(shortfall, cash_remaining) / open_price)`; skip if 0.
- Buys go to held names below target and to entry candidates (rank order)
  alike, whichever has the larger shortfall.
- No selling for rebalancing. Sells happen only through §4 exits and §9
  data events.
- Uninvested cash sits in a liquid sleeve accruing `cash_rate_annual`
  (declared flat 6% p.a.; a real liquid-fund or T-bill series may replace
  it only as a declared data upgrade, before any run).

## 6. Trend filter (the crash rule)

- `RISK_OFF(M)` if Nifty 50 close(M) < simple mean of the last 10
  month-end closes, including M (price index, not TRI).
- When RISK_OFF: no equity buys. The SIP accumulates in the liquid sleeve.
  Holdings are **not** sold. §4 exits still happen.
- When RISK_ON returns: all accumulated cash becomes investable under the
  normal 6-names-per-month rule.
- Exactly one version of this rule is the main run. The sell-to-cash
  variant lives in the sweep (§12) as a stability check, not as an option.

## 7. Execution and costs

- Decisions use the close of month-end M. Orders fill at the open of the
  next session M+1. Reference price = close(M); fill = open(M+1); the
  difference is slippage and is recorded per trade.
- Costs from `configs/costs_zerodha.toml`: zero brokerage, STT 0.1% each
  side, exchange transaction charge, SEBI fee, stamp duty on buys, GST on
  the applicable charges, DP charge per scrip sold.
- No market-impact model. Guard: halt the run if any single buy exceeds
  1% of that name's trailing 20-session average traded value.

## 8. Taxes (India, today's regime applied to every year)

- Policy: `configs/tax_policy_in.toml`. FIFO lots per ISIN (the rule for
  demat holdings).
- STCG 20% if held 12 months or less. LTCG 12.5% on the FY's net LTCG
  above 1,25,000 if held more than 12 months. Cess 4% on tax.
- Set-off: STCL against STCG then LTCG; LTCL against LTCG only.
  Current-year set-off first, then brought-forward losses, oldest first.
  Carry forward 8 years.
- Dividends taxed at slab (declared 30% + cess), reinvested to the cash
  sleeve on ex-date.
- Grandfathering and pre-2024 rates are ignored: this is a policy
  counterfactual, chosen because the portfolio will live under the
  current regime.
- Liability for FY ending 31 March is deducted from the cash sleeve on the
  first session of the new FY, before that month's buys. A negative cash
  balance is filled by subsequent SIPs before any buy; it accrues nothing.
- Two after-tax numbers are reported: running after-tax NAV (taxes
  actually paid) and **liquidation-adjusted terminal value** (everything
  sold on the last session, taxed in that FY). Primary metrics use the
  liquidation-adjusted basis; deferral is a real advantage but must be
  paid for at the end.

## 9. Data rules

- Prices adjusted for splits and bonuses by corporate-action factors.
  Dividends are cash on ex-date × shares held.
- A security whose bhavcopy rows stop (delisting, suspension) exits at its
  last available close on the session after its last row, with normal
  sell costs. A sweep row assumes −100% instead.
- Plausibility halts: a one-session move beyond ±40% not explained by a
  corporate action; a missing session for a held name; a fill price of 0.
  Findings that merely look odd are recorded in the run manifest.
- Point-in-time: decisions on M use only rows dated <= M. Universe, ranks
  and filters are computed on the panel truncated at M.
- Split and bonus factors are derived from the bhavcopy's adjusted
  `PREVCLOSE` on ex-dates (verified on known events), not from a
  corporate-actions feed. Dividends come from the NSE corporate-actions
  feed, including names since delisted. Dividend rows that cannot be
  joined to a panel ISIN are dropped and counted in the manifest.
- Pre-2011 bhavcopies have no ISIN column; symbols are mapped to ISIN via
  the symbol-change history. Unmapped rows are dropped and counted in the
  manifest.
- Symbol continuity: pre-ISIN renames are stitched only from a verified list
  where the corporate-actions feed shows the same company name on both sides
  of the change. Mergers and schemes of arrangement are not renames and
  follow the delisting rule above.
- A corporate-action factor derived from `PREVCLOSE` (an action whose PURPOSE
  text carries no ratio) is applied to a held position as a **cash credit** of
  (prior close − adjusted prev close) × shares held, on the ex-date. The lot's
  cost basis is reduced in the same proportion as the price. It is not a sale:
  no gain or loss is realised and no tax event occurs. The cash re-enters
  through the normal §5 buy rules. Share count is unchanged.
- Where a `PREVCLOSE`-derived factor and a dividend fall on the same ex-date
  for the same ISIN, only one applies: the dividend when the implied price
  drop is explained by it, otherwise the factor. The count is recorded in the
  manifest.

## 10. Benchmarks (identical SIP schedule, cash sleeve, costs and tax engine)

Each index is modelled as a single security bought monthly, never sold,
taxed on liquidation.

- B1 Nifty 50 TRI
- B2 Nifty 500 TRI — primary benchmark
- B3 Nifty 200 Momentum 30 TRI with 0.30% p.a. expense drag — the
  do-nothing alternative
- B4 Equal-weight of the E1 eligible universe, rebalanced monthly, with
  costs and taxes (shows what the universe itself does)

## 11. Evaluation periods (`configs/evaluation_periods.toml`)

| period       | window                     | rule                                    |
|--------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| warmup       | 2005-01-01 to 2006-12-31   | signal history only, no trading         |
| development  | 2007-01-01 to 2016-12-31   | open                                    |
| validation   | 2017-01-01 to 2021-12-31   | requires a written development review   |
| sealed       | 2022-01-01 to 2025-12-31   | one reveal, written reason, journaled   |
| live         | 2026-10-01 onward          | paper trading via Kite Personal         |

Every backtest starts with an empty portfolio at its period start, so
validation is not contaminated by development holdings. The data loader
truncates at the period end before the engine sees anything.

## 12. Pre-registered sweep (all rows run together; none is promotable)

Base row: lookback 12-1, N=25 / band 50, 6 buys per month, SMA-10 filter,
costs ×1, cash 6%, delisting at last close.

| row | change                                                       |

## 13. Success criteria (computed by `pflab classify`, never typed in)

On the liquidation-adjusted after-tax basis, in **both** development and
validation:

- **C1** CAGR(E1) − CAGR(B2) >= 2.0 percentage points per year (time-weighted, unitized NAV)
- **C2** CAGR(E1) − CAGR(B3) >= 2.0 percentage points per year
- **C3** MaxDD(E1) <= MaxDD(B2) + 5 percentage points
- **C4** C1–C3 hold in at least 6 of the 9 sweep rows
- **C5** (reported, not gating) stationary block-bootstrap 90% interval
  of monthly excess return vs B2 over development+validation

Verdict:
- **ADVANCE** if C1–C4 hold in both periods
- **REJECT** if C1 fails in both periods
- **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise

ADVANCE unlocks three things and nothing else: one sealed-period reveal
request, the start of live paper trading, and the start of E2.
INCONCLUSIVE and REJECT get a written diagnostic note. Neither gets a
parameter tweak.

## 14. What E1 does not claim

Not intraday. Not F&O. Not IPOs. No market-impact model. No prediction of
events. Not a forecast of anything; a set of rules whose historical
behaviour is being measured.
