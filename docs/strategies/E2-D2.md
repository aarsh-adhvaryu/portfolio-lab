**Change-log entries that carry over from E2-D** (read with §9): the

## 1. Purpose
Test whether excluding names whose insiders have been heavy net sellers,
by the SEBI Prohibition of Insider Trading disclosures, improves E1's
after-tax return or drawdown.

## 2. Hypothesis
Names where insiders sold, net of their buying, more than 1% of market cap
in the trailing two quarters underperform after costs; excluding them
improves E1.

## 3. Rule (the one change)
- **Net insider value at M** = the value of insider **sells minus buys**
  in PIT disclosures disseminated in the trailing two quarters as of M.
- **Denominator: market cap at M** = **total** shares outstanding × the
  close at M. Shares outstanding is the **reported** figure
  (`shares_outstanding_reported`) from the latest shareholding filing
  usable at or before M. **Free float exists nowhere in this project**
  (the finding that closed E1c §6), so total shares are used; declared.
- **Ineligible at M** if net insider value exceeds **1.0%** of market cap.
- **A held name that trips the rule exits** at that month-end's decision,
  sold at the next open, through E1's existing "ineligible" exit.
- **Point-in-time key**: the disclosure's dissemination timestamp in the
  NSE feed (the after-15:30 rule as E2-A §3: usable from the next session).

## 4. Definitions to settle at lock (not decided in this draft)
To be filled from the PIT feed when E2-D2 is built, and each confirmed by
Aarsh:
1. "Trailing two quarters" as the six calendar months ending at M, or the
   two most recent completed calendar quarters.
2. Which person categories count as insiders (promoters, promoter group,
   directors, KMP, designated persons, immediate relatives).
3. Which transaction modes count as buys and sells (market, off-market
   inter-se transfer, pledge creation/invocation, ESOP allotment, gift).
   Inter-se promoter transfers net to zero within a group and should not
   count twice.
4. Disclosures without a value, or with a quantity but no price.

## 7. Reported extras
Flags per month and how many E1 base held; forward 3- and 6-month return
of excluded names against the universe. **Bulk and block deals are
reported as unavailable**, not collected.

## 8. Not claimed
No tracking of specific investors. An insider sale can be for reasons
unrelated to the company.
