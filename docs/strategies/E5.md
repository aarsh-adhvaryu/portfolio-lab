# E5 — Learned signals

Status: DRAFT. Written now, run last. Judged by `GATE.md`. Each row below is
its own candidate against the adopted stack; only one may replace the
momentum rank, and only if it wins.

## 1. Purpose
Test whether a learned cross-sectional model, and then finance-native
foundation models, beat the momentum rank under the same harness.

## 2. Rows (each pre-registered here)

### E5-A LightGBM cross-section (the serious candidate)
- Target: 1-, 3- and 12-month forward excess return over the universe median,
  three models.
- Features at month-end M, all point-in-time: momentum (1, 3, 6, 12-1),
  volatility (21, 63, 252), turnover ratios, distance from 52-week high,
  E2-A flag count, E2-B betas, E2-D positioning, sector dummies.
- Training: expanding window, purged (21-session gap) and embargoed CV,
  hyperparameters fixed on 2007–2012 and frozen.
- Use: composite score = average rank across the three horizons replaces
  the 12-1 rank in E1's rule. Band and everything else unchanged.

### E5-B Kronos (finance-native foundation model, MIT licence)
- K1: `Kronos-small` zero-shot, 21-session-ahead close forecast from the
  last 512 daily K-lines, ranked cross-sectionally, as the composite score.
  **Declared contaminated**: pre-trained on 45 exchanges including NSE
  through at least 2024; development and validation results are reported
  with that caveat and cannot be the basis for adoption. Clean evidence
  comes only from the live year.
- K2: Kronos-small architecture **retrained from scratch** on this panel's
  NSE K-lines with a yearly cutoff (train ≤ year t, forecast t+1, roll).
  Clean. This row is adoptable. Compute: feasible on a 12 GB GPU for the
  small (~25M) model.

### E5-C TimesFM-3 zero-shot
Research-only (non-commercial licence). Same contamination caveat as K1.
Reported, never adoptable.

### E5-D Graph model
GNN over the E2-B graph (companies, sectors, factors) predicting the same
targets as E5-A. Must beat E5-A to be considered.

## 3. Window
E1's full window for E5-A, K2, E5-D. K1 and E5-C on the same window with the
caveat.

## 5. Prior, written now
Small or no improvement over the momentum rank after costs and tax. Trees
beat foundation models on the same features in the published evidence. A
null result here is a result.

extraction with timestamps and recorded cutoffs.
