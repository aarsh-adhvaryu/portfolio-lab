# E2-D1 — Promoter selling

Status: **DRAFT** 2026-09-27. Split from E2-D by Aarsh (see `E2-D.md` §9).
Locks at its first development run. Judged by `GATE.md` against STACK =
E1 base re-run on this charter's window, and reported against MANDATE §3
and §5.

**Change-log entries that carry over from E2-D** (read with §10): the
2026-09-16 draft and the 2026-09-24 amendments to data and dating.

## 1. Purpose
Test whether excluding names whose promoters have been selling improves
E1's after-tax return or drawdown. Promoters know the company; heavy net
selling by them is the most direct disclosed signal of their view.

## 2. Hypothesis
Names whose promoters sold more than 1% of the company's shares over the
trailing two reported quarters underperform after costs by enough that
excluding them beats the momentum signal's occasional liking for them.

## 3. Rule (the one change)
- **Measure.** Promoter **shares** — not percent — in the latest usable
  quarterly shareholding pattern as of M, against the pattern two quarters
  earlier. *Why shares:* the rule is about "net promoter selling", and
  promoter percent also falls when the company issues new shares and
  promoters sell nothing.
- **Ineligible at M** if promoter shares fell by **more than 1.0% of
  shares outstanding** over those two quarters.
- **Pledge invocations** reduce promoter shares and **count** as selling
  (declared: the promoter lost the shares, whoever pressed the button).
- **A held name that trips the rule exits** at that month-end's decision,
  sold at the next open — through E1's existing "ineligible" exit, not
  intra-month.
- **Point-in-time.** A quarter is usable from its `filing_date` where one
  exists (December 2015 quarter onward), else from **the second month-end
  after its quarter end** (E2-D §4, 2026-09-24). Both quarters in the
  comparison must be usable at M; if the earlier one is missing, the name
  has no measure and is not flagged.

## 4. Definitions the rule needs (declared at draft, confirm at lock)
1. **Split and bonus adjustment.** A 1:1 bonus doubles promoter shares
   and a consolidation shrinks them, neither of which is buying or
   selling. The earlier quarter's promoter shares are restated in the
   later quarter's share units using the panel's own corporate-action
   factors between the two quarter ends.
2. **Denominator.** Shares outstanding at the **later** quarter:
   `shares_outstanding_reported` from the December 2015 quarter onward,
   `shares_outstanding_derived` (promoter shares ÷ promoter percent) before
   it (median derivation error 0.009%, `docs/notes/E2D-readiness.md`).
3. **"Two reported quarters"** = the change between the latest usable
   quarter and the quarter two before it (two quarterly steps), both
   usable at M.
4. **Promoters who sold everything before 2016.** The pre-2016 endpoint
   lists promoter entities only; a company with no promoter row is
   recorded unavailable, not zero. A promoter group's complete exit in
   that era therefore shows as a missing measure and is not flagged.
   Declared; the reverse error (flagging a non-seller) cannot occur.

## 5. Data
`data/bse_shareholding/summary.parquet`: 135,098 usable scrip-quarters,
2010–2026. Promoter shares are present in **100%** of usable rows in both
eras (checked 2026-09-27), so the rule is supported across the window.
Scrip code → ISIN via `data/bse_scrip_isin/map.parquet`.

## 6. Window
Development **2010-01-01 .. 2016-12-31**. Validation **2017-01-01 ..
2021-12-31**. E1 base re-run on both windows as STACK. Sealed untouched.

The first measure needs three quarters (March, June, September 2010), the
last usable from 2010-11-30 under the second-month-end convention, so the
rule is inert for the first ten development months. Declared.

2010–2015 is dated by convention and 2016 onward by filing date; S3 and S4
price that difference.

## 8. Reported extras
Promoter-selling flags per month and how many E1 base held; forward 3- and
6-month return of excluded names against the universe; how many flags came
from pledge invocations. **Bulk and block deals (E2-D §7) are reported as
unavailable**: they were never collected, and are not collected for this
charter.

## 9. Not claimed
No tracking of specific investors. A promoter sale can be for reasons
unrelated to the company (tax, estate, diversification); the rule does not
distinguish them.

- 2026-09-27 (Aarsh, session 1 of 4) — **§3 flag rule settled before lock.**
  A name is flagged only when promoter shares fell by more than 1.0% of
  shares outstanding **and** promoter percent fell by more than 1.0 pp over
  the same window. *Reason:* consolidation lowers shares without selling; an
  issue lowers percent without selling; selling lowers both. Answers the
  readiness note's share-count problem (16% of flags). Recorded only; not
  yet built or locked.
