# E2 — Event layer (pre-defined, not yet chartered)

E2 is three separate additions to E1. Each is tested **alone**, on top of
the E1 base row, under E1's periods, sweep and success criteria, and is
adopted only if it improves C1 or C3 in both development and validation
without breaking the other. Adopted components stack in the order they
were adopted. E2 does not start until E1 returns ADVANCE, but E2's data
collection starts on day one because the live-forward period needs it.

## E2-A Firm-level red flags -> exclusion and exit

Hypothesis: names with a red-flag announcement in the trailing 12 months
have negative drift after costs; excluding them (and exiting held names
on a new flag) improves after-tax CAGR or drawdown.

- Source: BSE and NSE corporate announcement archives. Use the
  dissemination timestamp, not the news date, as the point-in-time key.
- Flag types (keyword rules in `pflab/data/red_flags.py`, to be refined
  against the backfill before any test): auditor resignation, regulatory
  action (SEBI/SAT/ED/CBI), forensic audit, default or rating cut to D,
  promoter pledge disclosure, results delay, insolvency (NCLT/IBC), fraud.
- Rule under test: flagged names are ineligible for 12 months; a held
  name is sold at the next open on a new flag.
- Testable immediately on backfill: thousands of events across hundreds
  of names is a real sample.

## E2-B Shock-exposure graph -> portfolio constraint

Hypothesis: capping the portfolio's beta to any single shock factor
reduces drawdown in 2008, 2013, 2020 and 2022 without giving up CAGR.

- Shock factors: Brent crude, USDINR, US 10-year yield, FII net flows,
  India VIX, and a GDELT-derived geopolitical intensity series.
- Edges: rolling 36-month betas of each name (and sector) to each factor,
  estimated point-in-time, stored as a typed graph
  (company -> sector, company -> factor with beta weight).
- Constraint under test: no factor's portfolio beta may exceed 1.5× the
  Nifty 500's beta to that factor; buys that would breach it are skipped
  for the next candidate.
- Testable immediately on backfill: 30 years of prices and macro series.

## E2-C Event-intensity regime input

Hypothesis: GDELT tone/count features plus India VIX give a better
RISK_OFF signal than the plain SMA-10 rule.

- Declared up front: 11 years of GDELT 2.0 contain about four stress
  episodes. This can never be proven statistically. If adopted it is
  adopted as a risk rule held on priors, and reported as such.
- Test: replace §6 of E1 with the composite rule; adopt only if drawdown
  improves in both periods with no more than one extra RISK_OFF switch
  per year on average (whipsaw guard).
